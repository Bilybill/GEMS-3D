01 / The idea

Different measurements.
The same Earth.

A subsurface structure can look very different through gravity, magnetic, electrical, and seismic measurements. Studying these views together calls for data that share the same geological context.

GEMS-3D pairs three-dimensional property volumes with observations from all four modalities. Spatially aligned properties, anomaly labels, and acquisition metadata connect physical structure to its observed signatures.

Use these paired representations to study supervised forward surrogates, joint property reconstruction, anomaly recognition, and robustness to incomplete observations.

03 / Research directions

A benchmark with more than one view.

Use a common dataset to ask how physical information is learned, combined, and recovered.

I.

Learn the forward map

Predict geophysical observations from their corresponding property volumes, or learn all four mappings jointly.

Physical properties → Observations

Forward surrogate benchmarks ↗
II.

Recover the subsurface

Reconstruct aligned property volumes from multiple observations, or recognize the underlying anomaly family.

Observations → Subsurface properties

Joint inversion baseline ↗
III.

Test what each view contributes

Evaluate missing modalities and shuffled cross-modal pairings to examine robustness and the contribution of aligned information.

Complete views ↔ Incomplete views

Robustness evaluation ↗
